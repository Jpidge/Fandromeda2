=================================================
PRIMARY PRODUCT OBJECTIVE
=================================================

FANDROMEDA's ultimate objective is NOT merely:

    "Predict fantasy points."

The objective is:

    Produce fantasy-football decisions that are demonstrably better than
    commonly available baseline information, especially Yahoo Fantasy's
    projections.

The system should eventually be able to answer:

1. How many fantasy points will this player score this week?

2. How confident are we?

3. WHY does the model believe that?

4. Is our projection materially different from Yahoo's?

5. Historically, when this type of disagreement occurs, which model tends
   to be correct?

6. Should the fantasy manager start, bench, add, drop, hold, or monitor
   this player?

7. Would following FANDROMEDA rather than Yahoo historically have resulted
   in more fantasy points and/or matchup wins?

The long-term scientific question is:

    "Does FANDROMEDA outperform Yahoo Fantasy projections and decisions?"

Call this benchmark internally:

    THE FANDROMEDA CHALLENGE

=================================================
CRITICAL MODELING PRINCIPLE — NO TARGET LEAKAGE
=================================================

Prevent target leakage at all costs.

When predicting player P in Week W:

    NOTHING that became known during or after P's Week W game may be used.

Features must represent information available before prediction time.

Examples of valid historical features:

    Week W-1 usage
    Week W-1 fantasy production
    rolling statistics through W-1
    pregame injury information
    known depth chart
    known matchup
    known schedule
    known betting information, if later incorporated and timestamp-valid

Invalid:

    Week W result
    final Week W statistics
    injury information published after Week W kickoff
    revised projections produced after kickoff
    any feature indirectly calculated using target-week outcome

Every ML/backtesting change must be reviewed specifically for temporal leakage.

Inspect the current feature generation carefully.
